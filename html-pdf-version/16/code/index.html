Solution Activities Output Metrics Community Impact Metric Targets
Advocate for and Provide Better Resources and Support for Those Experiencing Mental Illness
  • Engage in community councils to advocate for mental health equity and leverage data for impactful action.
  • Support the implementation phase of the Mental Health Court in collaboration with local organizations.
  • Engage with Florida Mental Health Advocacy Coalition (FLMHAC) to support legislative priorities to improve the lives of those with Mental Illness and their families.
  • Number of advocacy events or meetings held with community councils and stakeholders focused on mental health equity.
  • Number of individuals with mental health conditions who received legal representation through the Legal Defense Fund.
  • $ distributed through the Legal Defense Fund.
  • Number of legislative priorities and wins.
  • Percentage of individuals with mental health conditions reporting improved access to resources.
  • Number of laws enhanced or changed.

Educate the Community
  • Host forum meetings, seminars, and public awareness events using storytelling to evoke empathy.
  • Partner with the press and utilize social media to amplify the stories of those affected by mental illness and incarceration.
  • Conduct collaborative book reviews to promote awareness and discussion of relevant topics.
  • Number of community members attending forums, seminars, and public awareness events.
  • Number of media stories or social media posts shared that highlight mental health and incarceration issues.
  • Percentage of attendees who report increased understanding and empathy toward individuals experiencing mental illness and incarceration after participating in educational events.
 75%
Support Families with Access to Mental Health Services
  • Distribute free therapy vouchers to underserved families.
  • Host social events to provide socialization and foster supportive environments.
  • Provide financial and advocacy support for individuals with mental health conditions navigating the justice system through a Legal Defense Fund.
  • Number of therapy vouchers distributed to underserved families.
  • Number of attendees at mental health resource events hosted by the organization.
  • Number of partners & volunteers engaged at safe space events.
  • Percentage of families reporting improved mental well-being and access to support services within six months of receiving therapy vouchers or attending events.
 75%
