Problem (Opportunity)

Problem Statement:

In our nation, the heartbreaking reality is that jails and prisons have become the largest providers of psychiatric care—places never designed to offer the compassionate treatment that people living with mental illness so desperately need. The systems meant to support this vulnerable population—medical, social, and legal—are a fragmented patchwork, riddled with bureaucracy and lacking the coordination necessary to meet their complex needs. It is imperative that we ensure access to holistic and vital care services to safeguard their well-being and restore dignity to those caught in this cycle of neglect.
