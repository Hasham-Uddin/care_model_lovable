Dear Friend,

As we work to eliminate social justice disparities, Measure calls for strengthening communities to self-advocate through our CARE Model.

Community Mobilization (CM) is defined as a process of building the capacity of communities to plan, carry out, and evaluate activities in a participatory and sustained way to improve their health. CM aims to facilitate positive and sustainable changes in social norms and attitudes and in individual, household, and community practices.

By centering anti-racism and community voice, the CARE model improves the community mobilization process. Without an equitable framework like the CARE Model built into the process of community mobilization, we risk the continual perpetuation of oppression. We might even do harm rather than good in our neighborhoods.

This CARE Model Community Mobilization Guide intends to provide direction on how to partner with your community to participate in the process of change fully. The strategy includes both the problem (opportunity) and solutions to fix it. A detailed theory of change is formed with a few key metrics to measure outcomes.

When you are ready to put this plan into action the strategy recommends using a transparent system to track and measure progress to make the approach sustainable.

Measure hopes this community mobilization guide will encourage your organization to move forward in the elimination of social disparities, innovate for justice, and measure world-changing solutions along the way.

Cheers To The Greater Good,

Meme Styles

Measure Founder + President
