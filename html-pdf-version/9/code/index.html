ENGAGEMENT AND STRATEGY

“Stakeholder” refers to people, individuals, organizations and groups that are interested in supporting your nonprofit.

Stakeholders might include people directly involved like board members, or local business that might benefit from your service to the community. The Stakeholder wants to see you succeed.
