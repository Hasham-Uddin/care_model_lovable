MISSION

Patients Not Prisoners reached out to Measure to provide guidance and data that would support the creation of programs that shift the experiences of people living with a mental illness. Patients Not Prisoners seeks to reduce the criminalization of mental illness in Putnam & St. Johns County, FL. Many of the people incarcerated sit at the intersection of racism, poverty, and mental illness.

SOLUTIONS

Patients Not Prisoners offers a variety of programs and services for the mentally ill based on the CARE Model principles.

  • ADVOCATE for and provide information regarding the issues of the mentally ill impacted by the legal system.
  • EDUCATE the community about mental health resources and tools to assist families.
  • SUPPORT families with programs and services during and after their interaction with law enforcement/the Courts.
