MEASURING SUCCESS

This plan can chart the path towards collecting the data needed to measure your impact and also illustrate who is accountable.

COMMUNITY IMPACT METRIC

This heart indicates the metrics that are impacting people, behavior, laws and creating community change. These are considered our Community Impact Metrics.

% of individuals with mental health conditions reporting improved legal outcomes and access to resources. % of attendees who report increased understanding and empathy toward individuals experiencing mental illness and incarceration after participating in educational events. % of families reporting improved mental well-being and access to support services within six months of receiving therapy vouchers or attending events.
