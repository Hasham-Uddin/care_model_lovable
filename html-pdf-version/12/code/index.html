Solutions Alignment

COMMUNITY FIRST SOLUTIONS

Solutions alignment ensures that your organization’s high-level solutions (or strategies) will be in a position of agreement or alliance with what your community needs. The solutions alignment process helps to ensure the CARE Team has solutions that are equitable, feasible, desirable and viable to the community and target population.
