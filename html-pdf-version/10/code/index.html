WHAT IS A DATA COLLECTION PLAN?

A Data Collection Plan is a clearly defined approach to gathering both baseline data/information as well as data that can give your organization insight into root causes.

This plan includes what data the organization needs to collect, how to collect it, and who will collect it.

Note: Keep in mind privacy rules when collecting and maintaining data.
